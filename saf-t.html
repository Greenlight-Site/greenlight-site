About SAF-T

What Is SAF-T?

Sensation Awareness Focus Technique (SAF-T) is a specialized training program that equips professionals with powerful de-escalation skills — rooted in the same principles behind Greenlight's core method.

SAF-T uses visualization techniques and guided eye movements to calm emotional distress and address the heightened arousal that makes crisis situations dangerous — for everyone involved.

Unlike approaches that rely on repeated discussion or medication, SAF-T works with the brain's natural processing ability to produce quick, lasting change — even in acute situations.

The SAF-T acronym

S Sensation — awareness of physical response
A Awareness — of what's happening in the body
F Focus — directing attention intentionally
T Technique — applied tools for immediate calm
