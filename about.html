About Us

We're Greenlight —
and we're here to help.

A team of certified practitioners in Mesa, AZ dedicated to helping people overcome trauma, PTSD, anxiety, grief, and more — quickly, privately, and without having to relive the past.

Our Mission

Get you to a better place as efficiently as possible.

Our Method

The ART method — proven, fast, and practitioner-guided.

Our Location

Mesa, AZ — valley-wide network of practitioners.

Who We Are

We believe relief
shouldn't take years.

Greenlight was founded on a simple belief: that people who are suffering shouldn't have to wait months or years to feel better. Too many people spend too much time in approaches that scratch the surface but never quite reach the root.

We use the ART method because it works — and because it respects the people who come to us. You don't have to tell us your story. You don't have to relive the worst moments of your life. You just have to show up.

We do the rest together.

"I made more progress in one hour than I had made in 3 years."

What We Stand For

Our Values

Every decision at Greenlight comes back to these.

⚡

Speed with depth

Fast results don't mean shallow ones. Our approach is designed to create real, lasting change — not just temporary relief.

🔒

Your privacy is sacred

You never have to share more than you want to. The process works even when the details stay completely private.

🎯

You stay in control

Our practitioners guide — but every choice belongs to you. Always.

🧠

Science-informed practice

The ART method is backed by peer-reviewed research and used by organizations including the U.S. military. We don't guess — we use what works.

🤝

No judgment, ever

Whatever you're carrying — trauma, grief, addiction, fear — we meet you exactly where you are. No labels, no diagnosis required.

🌱

Respect for your time

We don't create dependency. The goal is always to get you to a better place as efficiently as possible — and let you get on with your life.
