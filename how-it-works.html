How It Works

Finally Feel Like
Yourself Again

Greenlight uses a proven method that helps you access difficult memories, change how they're stored, and find lasting relief — often in just 1 to 5 sessions.

"I can't explain it, but there was something about the process — guided by my practitioner — that in the end left me feeling quite a bit like my old self."

— Greenlight client
Step 1

Whenever We Recall an Emotional
Memory, It Becomes Open to Change

This happens all the time without us even recognizing it. The power of Greenlight's method is in accessing the memory to intentionally open it up for change.

We can then take this opportunity to make the changes that will free us from the troubling images and sensations and the emotions that cause suffering.

Unlike approaches that ask you to talk through painful experiences over and over, this process works directly with the memory itself — making change possible in a fraction of the time.

You don't have to relive your pain to heal from it. You just have to locate it — your practitioner guides the rest.

Photo
Step 2

Eye Movements Enhance Your
Ability to Change Memories

Why eye movements work

🌙
Mimics natural sleep processing

Back-and-forth eye movements simulate the brain's natural state for processing emotional memories — the same state that occurs during deep sleep.

🔓
Opens the memory to change

This temporarily flexible state is exactly when new associations can be made — allowing you to replace what's painful with something you choose.

😌
Naturally calming

The movement itself has a relaxing effect — many clients report feeling calmer during the process than they expected.

Sets of smooth, back-and-forth eye movements are used to enhance your brain's natural ability to make positive changes to memories — and to relax you in the process.

This isn't hypnosis, and it isn't medication. It's a guided, conscious process where you remain fully aware and in control the entire time.

Your practitioner directs the movements while you focus on the memory — together, you move through it at a pace that works for you.

Step 3

Make Any Changes You Want to the
Images Connected to Your Memories

Photo

During your session, your practitioner guides you to re-envision the experience and direct a new narrative. You imagine replacing images and sensations associated with the difficult memory with your own choice of images — ones that feel more pleasing and positive.

After engaging in this process for a relatively short amount of time — typically 1 to 5 sessions — positive, long-lasting changes are made.

While you retain the ability to recall the facts of what happened, you no longer experience the intense emotions previously associated with it.

You keep the memory. You lose the pain.

The Full Process

Four Steps. Lasting Change.

01

Locate the memory

You focus on what's bothering you. No need to describe it in detail — your practitioner guides you to find it, not relive it.

02

Open it to change

Guided eye movements help your brain shift into a state where the memory becomes flexible and open to new associations.

03

Replace the images

You choose what replaces the distressing image. Your practitioner guides — but you decide. You stay in control the entire time.

04

Lasting relief

The memory stays but the emotional weight lifts. Most clients notice a real difference after just the first session.
