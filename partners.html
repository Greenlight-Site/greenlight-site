Why It Works for Organizations

Why Your Employees Will Prefer
(and Actually Use) Greenlight

Your employees' problems are affecting your organization's performance. Here's why Greenlight gets results where other approaches stall.

🔒

Lose the pain. Keep the knowledge.

Employees retain full memory of what happened — they simply no longer carry the emotional weight of it. No erasing, no hypnosis, no altering reality.

🩺

No diagnosis needed

Employees don't need a formal diagnosis or referral to start. If they're struggling, that's enough. Low barrier means higher adoption.

🤐

No need to relive the trauma

Unlike talk-based approaches, employees never have to verbalize or narrate their experience. Privacy is protected — and that builds trust.

⚡

Brief intervention

Most people see meaningful relief in 1–5 sessions. That means less time away from work, lower ongoing costs, and faster return to full performance.

🎯

They stay in control

The practitioner guides — but the employee decides what to replace, keep, and feel. This sense of agency dramatically improves completion rates.

📈

Measurable outcomes

Relief is immediate and lasting. Organizations see reduced absenteeism, improved morale, and better team performance — not just anecdotal stories.
