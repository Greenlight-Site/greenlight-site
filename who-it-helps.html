Veterans

Combat trauma & military PTSD

Veterans — especially those who have been in combat — have experienced trauma that can be difficult to address through talk-based approaches alone. Greenlight's method brings up traumatic experiences and changes the way the information is stored, ultimately replacing them with positive images. Used at the highest levels of the U.S. military.

Photo
First Responders

Cumulative trauma & on-the-job exposure

First responders sometimes suffer from cumulative exposure to traumatic incidents they encounter on the job. Greenlight can assist in bringing closure to these incidents, or help remove and replace images that have become stuck over time — restoring confidence and well-being.

Photo

Traumatic Events

One of the most successful applications of Greenlight's method is addressing trauma created by single incidents. Symptoms may include nightmares, avoidance of situations that bring back memories, or heightened reactions to ordinary situations.

Photo

Depression & Anxiety

People experiencing depression and anxiety can benefit from an approach that integrates cognitive and emotional processing. Greenlight's method works at the level of the underlying images and feelings — not just the surface-level thoughts — ultimately decreasing symptoms.

Photo

Addiction

Addiction can stem from self-medicating due to unresolved trauma. Greenlight's method can alleviate some of the underlying triggers and improve opportunities for lasting recovery — addressing the root, not just the behavior.

Photo

Grief & Loss

Losing someone — or something — changes everything. Greenlight can help you process grief in a way that honors the loss while releasing the emotional weight that's keeping you stuck. You don't have to talk about it to heal from it.

Photo

Relationship Issues

Strong emotions get in the way of resolving relationship issues. Greenlight can assist in processing negative experiences that trigger those emotions — improving your ability to approach issues with a calmer, clearer perspective.

Photo

Phobias & OCD

Phobias and obsessive patterns are often rooted in specific memories or associations. By targeting those at their source, Greenlight's method can reduce or eliminate the fear response that drives the behavior.

Why It Works

Find Hope & Relief
Regardless of What You're Carrying

No diagnosis required

You don't need a formal diagnosis to begin. If you're carrying something difficult, that's enough reason to reach out.

You stay in control

Your practitioner only guides the process. Every decision about what to replace, keep, or feel — is entirely yours.

No need to talk about it

You never have to verbalize your traumatic experience. The process works even when the details stay completely private.

Results in 1–5 sessions

Most people experience meaningful relief in just a handful of sessions — not months or years of ongoing work.

Works with physical challenges too

Greenlight's method has also been used to help people manage pain and physical symptoms that have an emotional or memory component.

Safe for adults and children

Greenlight's approach is gentle and adaptable — appropriate for a wide range of ages and backgrounds.
