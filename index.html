The Problem

The Simple Reason Traditional Approaches Fail

You may have tried other approaches in the past and been frustrated with slow or no progress. You're not alone.

Regular talk-based methods can feel very surface level. They focus on changing your thoughts but often fail to make the connection to your emotional and physiological responses.

These typical treatments require that you spend a great deal of time revisiting your traumatic memories — sometimes stuck there until your next session.

You're not letting go of your pain at that point. You're reliving it.
